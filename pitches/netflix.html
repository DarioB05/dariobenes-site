Thesis

Priced for a slowdown, built for profit: new leadership is running the 2022 playbook again, faster.

Netflix traded 46% below its November 2025 high at about 17x forward earnings, the same entry point as 2022, while operating income was up 22% for the year. The market was pricing engagement, not the business.

Thesis I, the engine still prints cash: through nine months of 2026, revenue grew 13.7% and operating income 21.6%. Operating margin rose from 26.7% in 2024 to 31.5% guided for 2026, and buybacks took 90% to 113% of free cash flow, cutting diluted shares 3.3% in a year.

Thesis II, new leadership: in one week of October 2026 Netflix unified content under Peter Rice, a Disney and Fox veteran who runs the full $20B budget including live sports, streamlined 6% of roles, and refocused 95% of the budget on core shows. Netflix reaccelerated after losing subscribers in 2022; this time it moved in one week.

Thesis III, four levers pull in 2027 and none needs new viewers: ads (over $1.5B in 2025, $2.7B guided for 2026, 4,000+ ad clients), tentpoles with theatrical windows, live sports, and aggregation of partner channels inside the app.

Street view vs our view

Where the team disagreed with consensus

The Street’s view

  • Growth has hit a wall, with no US net subscriber adds this year
  • Members are watching less, and more than 20 brokerages cut targets
  • Engagement is the business, and it has plateaued

Our view

  • The engine still prints cash: margins up, share count down 3.3%
  • New leadership ran the 2022-style reset in one week instead of a year
  • Four 2027 levers with dates and numbers, none needing new viewers

Valuation

Scenarios and price target

Scenario DCF. Returns from $63.42 with no dividend; probability-weighted target $76.96, a 21.3% return, with bull return to bear loss of 2.1x.

Bear case, 20% weight

$46.33 · (27.0%)
  • The reset fails: growth fades from 8% to 4%
  • Margin stalls near 32%, about the 2026 guide
  • 9.5% WACC: the market keeps a media multiple

Base case, 55% weight

$78.05 · +23.1%
  • No reacceleration: 11% growth in 2027, fading to 7%
  • Margin adds 1 point a year to 35.5%
  • 8.3% WACC: 2% churn and low debt

Bull case, 25% weight

$99.06 · +56.2%
  • Ads and the 2027 slate lift growth to 13%, fading to 9%
  • 38% margin by 2031 as ads scale on the same content cost
  • 8.0% WACC

Catalysts

Catalyst path

  • Nov to Dec 2026: the biggest live quarter yet, with NFL games on Thanksgiving Eve and Christmas and Fury vs Joshua
  • Jan 2027: Q4 report and the first 2027 guide under the new content leadership, the first test of the thesis
  • Early 2027: annual engagement report (view hours)
  • Mar 2027: ad tier launches in 15 more countries
  • 2027 slate: Narnia with a 45-day theatrical window, Bridgerton, and Wednesday

Risks

Key risks and mitigants

Engagement plateau

Risk: View hours grew about 2% per half, while viewing per subscriber fell about 9% over three years (Wells Fargo estimate).

Mitigant: Churn is about 2%, the lowest in streaming, and the bear case already fades growth to 4% ($46.33).

Ad ramp pace

Risk: The 2026 ad guide was reset from about $3B to $2.7B.

Mitigant: A lower bar to beat: ad load is 2.4 minutes per hour versus about 8, and 15 new ad countries launch from March 2027.

Discount rate and multiple

Risk: The target needs an 8.3% WACC versus a 10.7% CAPM rate; at 9.5% the base case is $62.89, about the entry price.

Mitigant: Buybacks retire about 3% of shares a year.
