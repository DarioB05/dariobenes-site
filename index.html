About

Curiosity for quantitative investing, built by iterating.

Financial Analysis and Management at Florida Gulf Coast University's Lutgert College of Business, minor in Economics, graduating December 2027. CFA Level I candidate for November 2026, SIE passed.

At Birch Creek Capital I research high-yield issuers, evaluating credit quality, covenants, and relative value to source additions to the firm's buy list.
